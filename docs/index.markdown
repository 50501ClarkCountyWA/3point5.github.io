## Thank You for Joining the April 5, 2025 "Hands Off" Demonstration!

On April 5, 2025, you took part in the "Hands Off" demonstration at Esther Short Park in Vancouver, Washington, alongside hundreds of passionate individuals who care about protecting democracy and resisting executive overreach. Whether you held a sign, joined a chant, or showed up in solidarity, your participation matters.

Accurate crowd counts help build momentum for change and strengthen our efforts to create a more just and democratic society.

## Why We’re Counting Protesters: The Power of Numbers

The Crowd Counting Consortium (CCC) tracks protest participation across the country, and every count helps demonstrate the growing momentum of dissent. These numbers are critical in showing decision-makers the widespread public demand for action, holding our leaders accountable, and contributing to efforts like Trump’s potential "irregular" exit from office.

By collecting accurate data, we show that the movement is not only real but expanding. The more protests and participants counted, the more pressure we can apply to bring about change. Your individual protest count is a small but essential piece of a much larger puzzle.

## How It Works: Your "We Count" Ticket

We handed out "We Count" tickets at the April 5 demonstration, and you received one. 

Here’s how it works:
* We hand out tickets the day of the action to try to get a better count of participants throughout the entirety of the event
* We take the final count and other details about the event and create a public source with the information
* At the beginning of the following month, we check to make sure the data is included in the CCC dataset 
  * If so, we plot the information on our Momentum tracker
  * if not, we submit corrections  

You’ll be part of a collective count that shows the strength of our numbers and our commitment to ending executive overreach and protecting democracy.

## How Accurate Data Helps the Movement

Accurate protest data does more than just count heads—it builds momentum:

* Mass x Velocity = Momentum: The larger the number of participants (mass) and the more frequent the protests (velocity), the greater the momentum we generate.
* Momentum leads to change: Increased momentum raises visibility, strengthens our ability to influence political decisions, and is a good indicator of an "irregular leadership exit" (i.e. Trump leaving office before 2028 elections). 

Every protester counted helps amplify our collective voice, ensuring that our message reaches further and impacts the decisions that shape our future.
